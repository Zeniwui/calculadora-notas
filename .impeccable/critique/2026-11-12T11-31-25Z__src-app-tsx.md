### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The design feels authored specifically for Spanish university academic grading rather than an off-the-shelf simulator. It faithfully integrates prerequisite attendance gates, continuous assessment trees, discrete exam minimums (*notas mínimas*), regulatory grade caps at 4.0 (*tope reglamentario*), and session carry-overs (*partes conservadas*). The visual identity ("Cuaderno de Cátedra") successfully conveys academic sobriety through paper tones (`#f8f6f0`), dark ink (`#1c2024`), and classic serif titles (`Lora`). However, the grade numerals are currently styled in generic `font-mono` rather than *Lora* or tabular *Inter*, introducing a slight terminal/debugger feel where an authoritative academic record is expected.

### Overall Impression
The interface has achieved an exceptional level of conceptual rigor and visual maturity: it looks like a serious university tool rather than a toy calculator. What doesn't work is interaction congestion in the evaluation rows on mobile devices, muted text contrast failing accessibility standards, and hiding the reason for a 4.0 penalty cap behind a collapsed drawer when a student is stressed. The single biggest opportunity is transforming the tool from a passive calculator into an active academic advisor that clearly prescribes the easiest path to pass.

### What's Working
1. **Academic Restraint & Gravitas ("Cuaderno de Cátedra")**: The paper-and-ink palette, serif headers, and strict zero-emoji discipline create a dignified, trustworthy atmosphere suited for high-stakes university coursework.
2. **Mathematical Transparency & Epistemic Auditability**: The step-by-step substitution in `ExplanationPanel` provides complete defense against arbitrary grading; every formula and intermediate weight is demonstrable.
3. **Target Solver with Grade Locking**: The inverse calculator dynamically solves for what a student needs while respecting locked grades, directly addressing the core anxiety of final exams.

### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Immediate Penalty Diagnosis (Unbury the "Tope 4.0" Rationale)
- **What**: When a regulatory minimum is breached, the final grade is capped to 4.0, but the reason is hidden inside the collapsed "Ver detalle del cálculo" accordion.
- **Why it matters**: Severe emotional valley. A student with an arithmetic average of 7.2 suddenly sees a failing 4.0 with no immediate inline explanation of which exam caused the penalty and what is needed to unlock it.
- **Fix**: Render a prominent notice banner directly beneath the hero grade whenever `isCappedByPenalty` is true: *"Tope reglamentario aplicado (4.0): Examen Práctico 1 tiene 3.50 (mínimo 4.00). Necesitas +0.50 para desbloquear tu media real de 7.20."*
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

#### [P1] Mobile Layout Congestion & Sub-44px Tap Targets in `ScoreInput`
- **What**: Title, weight, minimum tag, range slider, number input, lock button, and reset button are crammed into an unyielding horizontal flex row.
- **Why it matters**: On viewports ≤ 390px, sliders collapse into ungrabbable slivers and the lock/reset buttons become sub-22px tap targets, causing frequent mis-taps.
- **Fix**: Stack controls on mobile (`flex-col sm:flex-row`), give range sliders full row width on touchscreens, and ensure interactive hit areas are at least 44×44px.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

#### [P2] WCAG AA Text Contrast Deficit on Secondary Metadata
- **What**: Secondary labels, weights, and table captions use `#7d8590` on `#fffdfa` (3.7:1) and `#f8f6f0` (3.5:1), failing the 4.5:1 WCAG AA threshold.
- **Why it matters**: Low vision students and users reading screens in bright university libraries cannot easily read weight percentages, minimum tags, and disclaimers.
- **Fix**: Replace all instances of `#7d8590` with the specified `#636b74` (5.05:1 contrast) or darker `#4b5563`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### [P2] Destructive Action Safety Net (Replace `window.confirm` with Undo)
- **What**: "Limpiar notas" triggers a native browser `window.confirm()` popup that irreversibly deletes all entered grades.
- **Why it matters**: Accidental clicks wipe an entire semester of entered data with no recovery path, causing anxiety.
- **Fix**: Replace the blocking browser dialog with an in-app confirmation drawer or execute an optimistic wipe accompanied by a 6-second floating "Deshacer" (Undo) action.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P2] Typographic Alignment: Grade Figures in Lora / Tabular Lining
- **What**: The hero grade (`text-5xl font-black font-mono`) and subtotal grades use generic monospace rather than the design specification.
- **Why it matters**: Diverges from `DESIGN.md` (“Títulos y cifras de nota: Lora”), imparting an engineering terminal feel rather than an official academic certificate.
- **Fix**: Render hero grades in `font-display font-bold font-feature-settings: 'tnum'` (Lora with tabular numerals), restricting monospace strictly to formula variable abbreviations (`NT`, `NP`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

### Minor Observations
1. **Target Presets Noise**: Offering 5 separate preset buttons (`5.0, 6.0, 7.0, 8.0, 9.0`) plus a custom input creates 6 simultaneous choices, failing Cognitive Load dimension 6. Condensing to academic milestones (`5.0 Aprobado`, `7.0 Notable`, `9.0 Sobresaliente`) would reduce visual noise.
2. **Brand Logo Link**: Clicking the university crest or title in `Header.tsx` does not navigate back to the Dashboard.
3. **Decimal Input Regionalization**: Spanish students type `,` for decimals; `<input type="number">` can drop comma characters before state formatting runs.
4. **Dashboard Empty State**: If zero subjects are loaded, `Dashboard.tsx` renders a blank void without guidance or import assistance.
